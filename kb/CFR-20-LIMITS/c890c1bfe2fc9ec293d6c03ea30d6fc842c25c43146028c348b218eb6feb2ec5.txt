Sanitary sewerage means a system of public sewers for carrying off waste water and refuse, but excluding
sewage treatment facilities, septic tanks, and leach fields owned or operated by the licensee.
Self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) means an atmosphere-supplying respirator for which the breathing air
source is designed to be carried by the user.
Shallow-dose equivalent (Hs), which applies to the external exposure of the skin of the whole body or the skin of
an extremity, is taken as the dose equivalent at a tissue depth of 0.007 centimeter (7 mg/cm 2).
Site boundary means that line beyond which the land or property is not owned, leased, or otherwise controlled
