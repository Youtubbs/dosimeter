Bioassay (radiobioassay) means the determination of kinds, quantities or concentrations, and, in some cases, the
locations of radioactive material in the human body, whether by direct measurement (in vivo counting) or by
analysis and evaluation of materials excreted or removed from the human body.
Byproduct material means-

radiation incident to the process of producing or using special nuclear material;
