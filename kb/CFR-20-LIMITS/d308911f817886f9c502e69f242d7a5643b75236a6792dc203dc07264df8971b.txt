Particle accelerator means any machine capable of accelerating electrons, protons, deuterons, or other charged
particles in a vacuum and of discharging the resultant particulate or other radiation into a medium at energies
usually in excess of 1 megaelectron volt. For purposes of this definition, "accelerator" is an equivalent term.
