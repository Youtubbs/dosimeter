Working level month (WLM) means an exposure to 1 working level for 170 hours (2,000 working hours per year/12

Year means the period of time beginning in January used to determine compliance with the provisions of this part.
The licensee may change the starting date of the year used to determine compliance by the licensee provided that
the change is made at the beginning of the year and that no day is omitted or duplicated in consecutive years.
