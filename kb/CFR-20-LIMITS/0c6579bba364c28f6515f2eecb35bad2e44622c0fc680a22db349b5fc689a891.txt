Annual limit on intake (ALI) means the derived limit for the amount of radioactive material taken into the body of an
adult worker by inhalation or ingestion in a year. ALI is the smaller value of intake of a given radionuclide in a year
by the reference man that would result in a committed effective dose equivalent of 5 rems (0.05 Sv) or a
committed dose equivalent of 50 rems (0.5 Sv) to any individual organ or tissue. (ALI values for intake by
ingestion and by inhalation of selected radionuclides are given in table 1, columns 1 and 2, of appendix B to §§
20.1001-20.2401).
