Nationally tracked source is a sealed source containing a quantity equal to or greater than Category 1 or Category
2 levels of any radioactive material listed in Appendix E of this part. In this context a sealed source is defined as
radioactive material that is sealed in a capsule or closely bonded, in a solid form and which is not exempt from
regulatory control. It does not mean material encapsulated solely for disposal, or nuclear material contained in any
fuel assembly, subassembly, fuel rod, or fuel pellet. Category 1 nationally tracked sources are those containing
