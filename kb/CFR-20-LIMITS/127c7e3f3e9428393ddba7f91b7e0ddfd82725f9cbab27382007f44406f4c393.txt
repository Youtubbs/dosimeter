Derived air concentration-hour (DAC-hour) is the product of the concentration of radioactive material in air
(expressed as a fraction or multiple of the derived air concentration for each radionuclide) and the time of
exposure to that radionuclide, in hours. A licensee may take 2,000 DAC-hours to represent one ALI, equivalent to
a committed effective dose equivalent of 5 rems (0.05 Sv).
Discrete source means a radionuclide that has been processed so that its concentration within a material has
been purposely increased for use for commercial, medical, or research activities.
