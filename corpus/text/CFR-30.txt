Footnotes to Schedule A:
1 Values are given only for those materials normally used
as gases.

2 µCi/gm for solids.
Note 1: Many radioisotopes disintegrate into isotopes
which are also radioactive. In expressing the
concentrations in Schedule A, the activity stated is that
of the parent isotope and takes into account the
daughters.

Note 2: For purposes of § 30.14 where there is involved a
combination of isotopes, the limit for the combination
should be derived as follows:

Determine for each isotope in the product the ratio
between the concentration present in the product and the
exempt concentration established in Schedule A for the
specific isotope when not in combination. The sum of such
ratios may not exceed “1” (i.e., unity).
