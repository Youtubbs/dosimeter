Exposure ID: exp-0412
Worker ID: WKR-1047
Date: 06/18/2026

During a scheduled radiography operation, I deployed the source from the SENTINEL 880 Delta camera through the guide tube for an exposure. When I attempted to retract the source after the exposure, the source assembly did not return to its fully shielded position. Survey-meter readings indicated that the source was still outside the normal shielded position.

I immediately stopped work and notified the rest of the crew. We withdrew from the immediate area, maintained the restricted-area boundary, and prevented other personnel from approaching the equipment. I did not make another attempt to operate or move the equipment after identifying the problem.

The source assembly remained associated with the radiography equipment, and no radioactive material was reported lost or stolen. The equipment was secured and removed from normal service pending evaluation by authorized personnel. The equipment problem, survey readings, and possible personnel exposure were reported to the Radiation Safety Officer.
